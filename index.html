KILLER FEATURE · v0.12 · UNCENSORED 27B IN 11.5 GB

A 3-bit abliterated 27B that computes a Moon landing on a 24 GB MacBook

Qwen3.8-27B-Heretic in PV-MACaQ 3-bit passes all 5 rungs of the Official Ladder, lunar mission included, at 16.7 tok/s. The stock 4-bit build stops at 4/5 and 11.6 tok/s.

  • The task. Launch from Alcântara on 2027-09-29, a three-stage rocket, a four-body transfer, braking at the Moon and a throttled soft landing. Every constant is given; the reference values are hidden from the model.
  • The check. The grader runs the model's code: exhaust velocity from √(2ηQ) for three stages (3,366 / 4,010 / 3,943 m/s), Tsiolkovsky Δv of 5,063 + 6,177 + 3,724 = 14,964 m/s within 0.1%, and all four mission stages implemented. Result: 6/6 invariants.
  • The cost. One 8,192-token answer in 575 s at 14.2 tok/s, with 43% of drafted tokens accepted by prompt lookup. 11.5 GB of weights, 13.6 GB peak GPU memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Mac hardware and OS are required for Basalt v0.13.0?
Any Apple Silicon Mac (M1–M4, 16 GB to 256+ GB unified memory) running macOS 27+. Run the prebuilt binary in two lines without Xcode, or build from source with Xcode 27.0+ (Swift 6.4 + MetalToolchain). Zero Python, Conda or Docker at runtime.
Do my documents, audio recordings, or prompts ever leave my Mac?
Never by default: Basalt runs 100% locally offline. Even when you opt into budgeted cloud escalation in a Compose file, perceived document and audio text defaults to data: no-perceived and never leaves your Mac.
How does automatic RAM recommendation work across the 117 Compose profiles?
When opening Compose Profiles (⌥⌘C) in Basalt.app, ComposeMemoryTier.detect reads physical unified memory and pins the 4 starred primary profiles for your tier at the top while keeping all 117 canonical profiles (tier × archetype matrix, Inkling 2 Mini, TranslateGemma-4, FLUX.3 & Video Studio) searchable and selectable.
