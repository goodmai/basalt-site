Frequently Asked Questions

What Mac hardware and OS are required for Basalt v0.11.0?
Any Apple Silicon Mac (M1–M4, 16 GB to 256+ GB unified memory) running macOS 27+ with Xcode 27.0+ (Swift 6.4 + MetalToolchain when building from source). Zero Python, Conda or Docker at runtime.
Do my documents, audio recordings, or prompts ever leave my Mac?
Never by default: Basalt runs 100% locally offline. Even when you opt into budgeted cloud escalation in a Compose file, perceived document and audio text defaults to data: no-perceived and never leaves your Mac.
How does automatic RAM recommendation work across the 84 Compose profiles?
When opening Compose Profiles (⌥⌘C) in Basalt.app, ComposeMemoryTier.detect reads physical unified memory and pins the 4 starred primary profiles for your tier at the top while keeping all 84 profiles searchable and selectable.
